androidKVM connects directly to your PiKVM or NanoKVM over a local network or VPN. No hosted relay or app account is required.

Watch the computer screen and control its mouse with touch gestures. Use the Android keyboard with extra keys for Esc, Ctrl, Alt, Win/Command, arrows, F1–F12, and Delete.

Save multiple KVM profiles, use power controls and Wake-on-LAN, and lock the app with a PIN or biometrics. NanoKVM connections also support device settings and terminal access.

You need your own compatible PiKVM or NanoKVM device. Features vary by device and firmware version.
